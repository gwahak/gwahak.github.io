Games

A small collection of browser-playable games. All games run entirely in your browser (HTML5 / JavaScript), with no external dependencies.

Card Games
  • FreeCell – Classic FreeCell with MSVC deals and safe auto-moves.
  • TokkiCell – A FreeCell variant with Jokers and extended cascade rules.
  • Klondike – Klondike Solitaire with selectable draw modes.
Other Games (mobile-friendly)
  • Rolit – 2–4 players, 4-color Reversi variant.
  • 4mation – 2 players, 4-in-a-row game.
  • pixel – 2-4 players, 4-in-a-row or 3-in-a-row game.
  • connect4 – 2 players, classic 4-in-a-row + my 3-player variant
  • trexo – two-players, 5-in-a-row game using O/X dominoes.
  • segyunjeon – 2 players, 8x8 Ataxx variant.(with AI)
  • Quod – 2-players version with AI, 2- 6 players version w/o AI.
  • BuffaloChess – 2 players game.
